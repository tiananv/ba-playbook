# Paper Lantern Community Clinic: booking workshop

These fictional workshop notes are inputs to the workflow. All roles, services
and events below are invented. No patient records are included.

Facilitator: We are discussing booking administration, not clinical decisions.

Reception role: Show only appointment slots that are still available.

Visitor role: I need to choose a service before I choose an appointment time.

Reception role: If two visitors choose the same slot, only one booking can be confirmed.

Visitor role: If the slot has gone, tell me and let me choose another time.

Referral coordinator role: A referral starts as pending until a coordinator records an administrative review.

Referral coordinator role: Do not let the booking page decide whether a referral is clinically suitable.

Visitor role: After I cancel, show a cancellation reference on screen.

Reception role: A cancelled slot should become available again.

Facilitator: We have not agreed a retention period, reminder channel or timeout for reserving a slot.

Service owner role: Keep those as open decisions. Do not turn them into rules.
